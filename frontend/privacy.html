Slap the Abuser is a social-awareness game. Playing as a guest needs no account and sends no data anywhere; your best scores stay in your browser.

What we store if you log in with Facebook

  • Your app-scoped Facebook ID (an identifier that is only meaningful to this app).
  • Your first name and last initial (you can replace it with a nickname) and your profile picture link.
  • Your game sessions and verified scores, and in-game friend connections.
  • If you choose to enable it: the app-scoped IDs of Facebook friends who also play, kept for at most 24 hours and used only to build your friends board.
  • A session cookie (HttpOnly) that keeps you logged in for up to 7 days. We store only a hash of it.

We do not store your Facebook access token, email address or friend list beyond what is listed above, and we do not sell or share data.

Delete your data

  • In the game: open Log in / Account, then “Delete my account”. Everything above is removed immediately.
  • From Facebook: Settings → Apps and Websites → remove “Slap the Abuser”. Facebook then notifies us and we delete your data automatically.
  • By email: contact@example.com (placeholder – replace with a real address).
