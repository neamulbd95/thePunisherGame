Privacy Policy

Bangla Punisher · the-bangla-punisher.cc · Last updated: 26 October 2026

Bangla Punisher (“the game”, “we”) is a free social-awareness game that lets players voice their anger against child abuse, street snatching and sexual violence in Bangladesh by playing a cartoon game. It is run by an independent developer based in Bangladesh. This policy explains what the game stores, why, and how you can delete it. Contact: contact@the-bangla-punisher.cc.

1. In short

  • There is no sign-in with Facebook, Google or any other company, and no email address or password.
  • You can play as a guest and nothing is sent to us. If you want to save scores and unlock more of the game, you choose a nickname; we add a number tag (for example Rahim#4821).
  • We do not sell data, show advertising, or track you across websites.

2. Playing as a guest

Guest play sends no personal data to us. Your best scores and preferences (sound, chosen weapon) stay in your own browser (local storage) and can be cleared in your browser settings.

3. If you choose a nickname

DataWhy we need it
Your nickname and the number tag we assign (shown as Nickname#1234), and a friend code.To show you on the leaderboard and let friends find you.
A recovery code, which you see once. We store only a one-way hash of it, never the code itself.So you can continue as the same player on another device or after clearing your browser.
A device cookie (HttpOnly, secure) that keeps this browser signed in as your player for up to a year. We store only a hash of it.To remember you without a password.
Your game sessions and verified scores, unlock progress, and in-game friend connections.To run the game, the leaderboards and unlocks.

Please do not put personal information such as your real full name or phone number into a nickname, because nicknames are visible to other players. We do not collect your email address, phone number, contacts, location or any identifier from your device other than the cookie above.

4. What is public

Your Nickname#tag and best scores are visible to other players on the leaderboard, and on a score preview if you choose to share a score (section 6). Your friend code is shown only to you.

5. Who handles the data

We do not share your data with anyone except the hosting providers that run the game for us: Cloudflare (website), Render (server) and Neon (database). They process data on our behalf. Like any web server, these providers see your IP address when you connect; we use it only transiently to limit abuse (for example, too many sign-ups from one address) and we do not store it in the game's database. The website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google to download them. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

6. Sharing a score on Facebook (optional)

After a saved score you can press Share on Facebook. That opens Facebook's own share window in a new tab, and only then does your browser contact Facebook. The game does not load any Facebook code, does not log you in to Facebook and receives nothing from Facebook. What is shared is a link to a public page that shows your Nickname#tag, score and the villain. Facebook's handling of what you post is governed by Facebook's own policies.

7. How long we keep it, and how to delete it

  • We keep your player while it exists.
  • Delete it yourself: open the game, press the Nickname button, then Delete my player. Your nickname, scores, sessions, recovery code hash and friend connections are removed immediately and permanently.
  • Ask us: email contact@the-bangla-punisher.cc with your Nickname#tag and we will delete it.
  • Signing out only removes the cookie from that device; your player and scores remain until deleted. Without the recovery code you cannot sign back in as that player.

8. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Cookies are HttpOnly and secure; the session cookie and the recovery code are stored only as hashes. Scores are verified on the server. No system is perfectly secure, but we collect very little so there is little to lose.

9. Cookies and local storage

One strictly necessary cookie when you have a nickname, and your browser's local storage for preferences. No advertising or tracking cookies.

10. Children

The game deals with serious subjects and is not directed at children under 13. We collect no personal data beyond what is described above, and a nickname should never contain personal information. If you want a child's player deleted, contact us.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and announce material changes in the game.
